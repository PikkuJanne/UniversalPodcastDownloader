## Implementation

- Isolate HTTP resume decisions in `src/ResumePolicy.ps1`; require a matching strong ETag, exact offset/total, canonical representation and original/final request identities. Send Range/If-Range only to the stored final target, including through redirects. Reject unsafe headers before body writes.
- Persist strict bounded schema-1 sidecars through `src/ResumeStore.ps1` under the existing held archive writer lock. Verify the entire partial's length/hash through an exclusive ReadWrite handle. Flush media before atomic sidecar replacement. Checkpoint the first nonempty chunk, geometrically spaced progress, and success/caught network failures.
- Extend the existing recorded transfer transaction. Append only a complete validated 206 tail. Ignored/inconsistent ranges, changed validators/representation and 416 use at most one fresh GET per attempt, retaining old partials and exact sidecar snapshots. The existing outer retry policy remains the only retry owner.
- Corrupt identity/checkpoint state, missing partials and uncheckpointed crash tails stop for review without truncation or deletion. Weak/absent validators, Last-Modified alone and unknown length cannot establish resume eligibility. Full/empty partial checkpoints do not establish completion.
- Retire matching resume metadata after prepared history evidence and before no-overwrite final placement. Existing final media, unknown partials, legacy protections, preview, privacy and history schemas remain protected. No new runtime dependency or option.

[RESUME_POLICY.md](../RESUME_POLICY.md) specifies schema, stricter-than-RFC acceptance, checkpoint spacing, retention and crash limits. D049-D051 record the choices. Independent transport, store and transaction reviews found no blocking issue.

## Limits

Recovery needs exact durable byte evidence. An abrupt kill after uncheckpointed writes preserves the files for review; it does not silently truncate the tail. Strong ASCII ETags, known totals, canonical media types, identity encoding and complete-tail 206 responses are an intentional conservative subset of HTTP behavior. Old partials/snapshots can consume disk space and are not automatically removed. A fresh response without eligible metadata may leave an earlier active sidecar and its old partial preserved; final completion still comes only from history and verified final media.

No real archive, private feed, live publisher, UNC filesystem, hardware power loss, privileged filesystem attacker, full media decoder or publisher authenticity guarantee was tested or claimed. Feed discovery/selection changes remain UPD-0203. No merge, history rewrite, persistent settings change, release or publication.
